# Recruitment viewport and creature focus

The recruitment gallery fits one or two complete families in portrait using the actual remaining roster height. The minimum row height includes measured names, ranks, prices and borders, plus a 64 CSS pixel portrait. There are no phone-model or browser-toolbar checks. Landscape retains one full-height family. Resizing preserves the same relative tier and scroll position.

Recruitable and locked forms share a compact footer. The full selected name uses all available width and its font is fitted with measured text, without ellipses. The cost occupies its own line below the fixed stat table. A flexible spacer anchors the stat table, quantity and actions at the bottom. The required-building explanation has been removed. Locked quantities stay visible and disabled, and the price line shows their unit cost. Recruitable forms show the current total. The existing unlock action still opens the building interface. Short landscape viewports can scroll when controls physically cannot fit; the measured Huawei 716 × 335 viewport fits without an inner stat scrollbar.

Animated previews use an offline-generated body reference for every registered animation. `tests/native/CreaturePreviewAudit.inc` renders source poses against black and white mattes to measure body coverage and its weighted center, excluding empty DEF margins and dark ground shadows. `scripts/audit/creature-preview-focus.mjs` stores the first visible resting body's dimensions and the measured centers of the preview sequences. The resulting `config/mobile-creature-focus.json` is approximately 177 KB for 370 animation paths shared by 372 creature entries.

The zoom depends only on the resting body, never on the full path or the size of an attack. It targets 80% of preview height with a 95% width limit. Frame positions follow the body's measured center without changing zoom. Wide weapons and distant effects may extend outside the portrait; they cannot shrink the actor. The native renderer clips to the portrait viewport and handles source edges without stretching. No pixel analysis or whole-group decoding runs during playback. One reusable native canvas grows only as necessary; the browser reuses its atlas canvas and pixel buffer. Preview updates remain capped at 30 Hz. Inspection cycles through available idle, movement, attack, shot and special sequences. Actual combat animation scheduling is unchanged.

The native catalog guard measures the rendered resting silhouette and rejects a creature below 75% of the preview's limiting dimension (allowing for transparent padding and rounding). Browser tests also measure large colored body components for Gremlin and Goonk, so a large travel envelope cannot pass while its actor has become tiny.

Scaled canvas drawing now respects the source canvas region, so a cropped canvas is actually scaled from its crop rather than from the complete underlying surface.

Validation is recorded in `outputs/recruitment-focus-0.4.241/validation.json`. Browser fixtures and native maps are generated locally; no device identifiers, private maps, saves or temporary phone adjustments are included in the public bundle.

The isolated catalog audit covers 372 animated creatures across all 11 registered faction categories, including Goonk and neutrals, and 21,745 frames in the available preview groups. Four legacy NOT USED placeholders have no animation. Every rendered frame stays within the preview margins; fade/particle poses may contain less visible body. The public engine is checked to exclude the temporary native audit export.
