# Recruitment viewport and creature focus

The recruitment gallery fits one or two complete families in portrait using the actual remaining roster height. The minimum row height includes measured names, ranks, prices and borders, plus a 64 CSS pixel portrait. There are no phone-model or browser-toolbar checks. Landscape retains one full-height family. Resizing preserves the same relative tier and scroll position.

Recruitable and locked forms share a compact footer. The full selected name uses all available width and its font is fitted with measured text, without ellipses. The cost occupies its own line below the fixed stat table. A flexible spacer anchors the stat table, quantity and actions at the bottom. The required-building explanation has been removed. Locked quantities stay visible and disabled, and the price line shows their unit cost. Recruitable forms show the current total. The existing unlock action still opens the building interface. Short landscape viewports can scroll when controls physically cannot fit; the measured Huawei 716 × 335 viewport fits without an inner stat scrollbar.

Animated previews use an offline-generated camera for every registered animation. The calibration helper in `tests/native/CreaturePreviewAudit.inc` renders each source pose against black and white mattes and recovers body coverage from their difference, excluding empty DEF margins and dark ground shadows. `scripts/audit/creature-preview-focus.mjs` combines the native coordinates of the sequences actually played in inspection (holding, moving, melee, shooting, special moves and movement transitions). The resulting `config/mobile-creature-focus.json` is approximately 21 KB for 370 animation paths shared by 372 creature entries.

Each creature keeps one camera and one scale for its entire preview. Native offsets remain visible, so lunges, raised weapons and throwing poses are preserved. The complete motion envelope fits within 80% of the preview's limiting dimension. No pixel analysis or whole-group image decoding runs during playback. One reusable native canvas grows only as necessary, and the browser reuses its atlas canvas and pixel buffer. Preview updates are capped at 30 Hz to sample the original 100 ms animation frames more evenly. Inspection cycles through available idle, movement, attack, shot and special sequences, ensuring attacks appear regularly instead of being chosen randomly. Actual combat animation scheduling is unchanged.

Scaled canvas drawing now respects the source canvas region, so a cropped canvas is actually scaled from its crop rather than from the complete underlying surface.

Validation is recorded in `outputs/recruitment-focus-0.4.240/validation.json`. Browser fixtures and native maps are generated locally; no device identifiers, private maps, saves or temporary phone adjustments are included in the public bundle.

The isolated catalog audit covers 372 animated creatures across all 11 registered faction categories, including Goonk and neutrals, and 21,745 frames in the available preview groups. Four legacy NOT USED placeholders have no animation. Every rendered frame stays within the preview margins; fade/particle poses may contain less visible body. The public engine is checked to exclude the temporary native audit export.
