Original Blizzard artwork and attack sounds for Marine, Firebat, Zergling, Hydralisk, Zealot and Dragoon are extracted from the StarEdit archives explicitly requested by the project owner, using the pinned OpenBW engine. See experiments/crossover/SECURITY-REVIEW.md.

experiments/crossover/scripts/export-classic-art.mjs produces per-unit native idle, movement, attack and death frames, catalogue/adventure icons and attack WAVs. The classic turn-based renderer uses these original frames in VCMI animation groups; real-time battles render OpenBW sprites directly. No replacement HD artwork is claimed. These experimental creatures are excluded from random armies.
